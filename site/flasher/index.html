Before you start

What you need

ESP32-CAM board

An Ai Thinker ESP32-CAM. The board has no USB port of its own.

USB adapter

The ESP32-CAM-MB adapter is the easiest: plug the board in and connect a USB data cable. A USB-to-serial adapter works too.

Chrome or Edge

On Windows, macOS or Linux. Firefox, Safari and phones can't access USB devices.

Step by step

How to install

1

Connect the board

Put the ESP32-CAM on the MB adapter and connect it to your computer.

2

Click Install

Choose the adapter's serial port, usually named CH340 or USB-SERIAL, and confirm. Installing takes a few minutes.

3

Restart

The board restarts when the installer is done. If nothing happens, press the RST button.

4

Scan a Wi-Fi QR code

Show a Wi-Fi QR code to the camera. Your FRITZ!Box and most phones can show one for your network. The flash LED blinks while the board connects.

5

Open FritzGate

Go to http://fritzgate and click Connect to FRITZ!Box to enter your login. Then hold a thermostat's gauge to pick its reference sensor.

Troubleshooting

If something goes wrong

The installer can't connect to the board
Put the board into download mode: hold the IO0 button, press and release RST, then release IO0. Click Install again.
No serial port shows up
Try another USB cable, because some cables only carry power. If the port still doesn't appear, install the CH340 driver for the adapter.
I use a USB-to-serial adapter instead of the MB adapter
Connect GND to GND, 5V to 5V, the adapter's TX to U0R and its RX to U0T. Connect IO0 to GND, then power the board and click Install. When the installer is done, remove the IO0 wire and press RST.
Will updating delete my settings?
No, as long as “Erase device” stays unchecked. The installer only replaces the firmware and keeps your Wi-Fi, FRITZ!Box login and sensor bindings.
Can I use a different flashing tool?
Yes. Each release on GitHub includes a complete image, fritzgate-<version>-full.bin. Write it to address 0x0 with esptool or Espressif's Flash Download Tool. The complete image also erases saved settings. All releases
